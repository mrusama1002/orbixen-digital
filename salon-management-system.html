Overview

Booking a salon appointment
should be as easy as sending a text.

Most salons still run on phone calls, DMs, and paper diaries — leading to missed messages, double bookings, and no-shows. Orbixen is building a system where the client's entire booking journey happens inside WhatsApp, the app they already use every day, while the business side gets the structure of a proper management platform underneath it.

Core Features

Three portals. One system.

💬
WhatsApp Booking
Clients pick a service, choose a stylist, and confirm a time slot directly inside a WhatsApp chat — automated, 24/7, with instant confirmations and reminders.
🛠️
Admin Dashboard
Salon owners get a full control center: live calendar, service & pricing management, staff scheduling, revenue reports, and client history in one place.
👤
Salon Staff Portal
Each stylist logs into their own portal to view their day, manage availability, mark appointments complete, and track their own bookings and earnings.
📅
Smart Scheduling
Automatic conflict checks, service-based time buffers, and staff availability rules keep the calendar clean without manual double-checking.
🔔
Automated Reminders
WhatsApp reminders before every appointment reduce no-shows and keep clients informed without any manual follow-up from staff.
📊
Client & Revenue Insights
Track repeat clients, top-performing services, and daily/monthly revenue — giving owners real visibility into how the salon is actually performing.
How It Works

From "Hi" to booked appointment.

01
Client messages on WhatsApp
No app, no signup — just an existing WhatsApp chat with the salon.
02
Picks service & stylist
Guided chat flow shows available services, staff, and open time slots.
03
Instant confirmation
Appointment is locked into the calendar and confirmed automatically.
04
Staff & admin stay synced
The booking appears instantly on both the staff portal and admin dashboard.
