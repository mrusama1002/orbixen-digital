Overview

Faster checkout.
Clearer numbers.

Retail businesses running on manual registers or spreadsheets lose time and accuracy at every sale. This POS system centralizes checkout, stock, and reporting in one interface — so staff can ring up sales quickly while owners always know exactly what's selling and what's running low.

Modules

What the system covers.

🧾
Sales & Checkout
Fast cart-based billing with discounts, multiple payment methods, and instant receipt generation.
📦
Inventory Management
Real-time stock levels across products, with low-stock alerts to avoid running out mid-sale.
🏷️
Product & Category Management
Organize the full catalog by category, pricing, and variants for quick lookup at checkout.
📊
Sales Reports
Daily and monthly breakdowns of revenue, top products, and staff performance.
🔐
User Roles
Separate access levels for cashiers and admins, keeping sensitive data and settings protected.
🖨️
Receipts & Invoicing
Printable or digital receipts generated automatically at the end of every transaction.
